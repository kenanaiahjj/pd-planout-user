## Users

PlanOut serves event attendees, guests, team leads, organizers, and venue staff. Attendees use the app while deciding what to join, checking out, completing required registration details, and presenting Passport access at the venue. Organizers configure event requirements and need the attendee flow to make timing rules clear without creating extra support work.

## Product Purpose

PlanOut is an event registration and access prototype centered on PlanOut Passport. Purchases create registration records, while the persistent Passport QR is the primary identity and access layer for event day. Success means users understand what is paid, what still needs a form, and when their Passport is ready to scan.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and active. The interface should feel like a modern operating layer for local events: confident enough for payments and registration, light enough for casual participants, and precise about action-required states.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic ticketing language that makes individual e-tickets feel primary over Passport. Avoid decorative SaaS gradients, over-rounded nested cards, vague success copy, and flows that route users away from confirmation when the remaining task can be completed inline.

## Design Principles

- Passport first: event access resolves through the user Passport, with tickets as supporting registration records.
- One coherent checkout: before-checkout requirements, payment, and post-checkout forms should feel like one flow.
- Action states must be explicit: paid, pending form, processing payment, and ready to scan need distinct visual language.
- Organizer rules are respected: form timing can vary by event or item and should be understandable to the buyer.
- Keep operational surfaces calm: use restrained color, strong hierarchy, and familiar controls for repeated tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion-friendly transitions, 44px touch targets on mobile, and copy that remains clear for first-time event participants and invited guests.
